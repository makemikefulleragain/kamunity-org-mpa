Design, scope, and pitch a corporate volunteering engagement before you approach a business partner. Complete the planner online, then print it or export a Word-compatible document.

Privacy and safety This tool does not send your answers anywhere and does not save them in localStorage. If you are on a shared computer, export or print what you need, then use Clear form. Do not include client-identifying details unless you have permission and a clear reason.
Limits This is a planning tool, not legal, insurance, governance, cultural safety, or professional advice. Confirm volunteer coverage with your insurer, use proper consent processes, and get qualified advice for formal partnerships, secondments, IP, contracts, or significant financial exchange.

4A. Finding the right contact

4B. Outreach message

4C. Conversation guide

Lead with their people: "What do your team usually get out of volunteering days? What has worked, and what has not?"
Be honest about fit: "This engagement is good for teams who want to do something physical and see a direct result."
Name the exchange: "We get a job done that genuinely improves daily life. Your team gets a well-run day and a real connection to the work."
Close with a specific ask: "Which of the three dates works best?"
